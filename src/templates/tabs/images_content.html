Supported Extensions: .jpeg, .jpg, .jpe, .jfif, .png, .bmp, .dib, .tif, .tiff, .heic, .heif, .avif
For files that hold several images (e.g., TIFF), specify which ones to extract. Examples: • Images 2 to 8: '2-8' • First 10 images: '-10' • Image 10 onwards to the end of the file: '10-' • Images 3, 6, and 10 to 15: '3, 6, 10-15' • Leave empty to process ALL items in the folder.
