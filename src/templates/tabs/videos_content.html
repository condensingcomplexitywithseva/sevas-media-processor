Supported Extensions: .mp4, .mov, .avi, .mkv, .wmv, .webm
• SUMMARY: Extracts a fixed number of frames across the whole video (e.g., exactly 10 frames). • SAMPLING: Extracts frames at a fixed time interval (e.g., 1 frame every 5 seconds).
Format MUST be strict HH:MM:SS. Examples: • First 10 seconds: '00:00:00-00:00:10' • One minute to two minutes: '00:01:00-00:02:00' • Specific times: '00:00:15, 00:00:30, 00:00:45-00:01:00' • Leave empty to process the whole video.
SUMMARY Mode Settings
Total frames extracted uniformly across the video if Mode is SUMMARY. Must be >= 1.
Compares pixels to skip consecutive frames that look visually identical. Bounds: Must be >= 0.0.
SAMPLING Mode Settings
How many pictures to capture every second if Mode is SAMPLING. Must be > 0.0.
Absolute maximum number of images for a single video in SAMPLING mode. Must be >= 1.
Compares pixels to skip consecutive frames that look visually identical. Bounds: Must be >= 0.0.
